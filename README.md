# Privacy Policy for Dead Orbit X

**Effective date:** 16 August 2026
**Last updated:** 16 August 2026

Dead Orbit X ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") is a mobile game developed and
published by **JAMEEL137**.

This page explains what information is collected when you use the Game, how it
is used, and the choices available to you.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personally identifiable information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Postal address
- Payment information

You can play the entire Game without creating an account or signing in.

All game progress (score, coins, unlocked ships, upgrades, settings) is stored
**only on your device** and is never uploaded to us.

---

## 2. Advertising

The Game is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are delivered through:

- **Unity LevelPlay (ironSource)** — [Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)
- **Unity Ads and mediated ad networks** — [Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy)

These advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

- Device type, model, and operating system
- **Advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
- IP address and approximate, country-level location derived from it
- Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completions)

This information is used to deliver and measure advertising. We do not control
how third-party advertising partners process this data; please review their
privacy policies linked above.

---

## 3. Your Advertising Choices

When you first launch the Game, you are asked whether to allow **personalized
ads**. Your choice is applied to our advertising partners and stored on your
device.

**You can change this choice at any time:**
**Pause menu → PRIVACY & ADS**

- **Allow personalized ads** — ads may be selected based on your advertising ID.
- **Use non-personalized ads** — you still see ads, but they are not selected
  based on your advertising profile. The Game remains fully playable either way.

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time from your
device settings:
**Android → Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID**

---

## 4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use diagnostics provided through Unity services to keep the Game stable,
including:

- Crash and error reports
- Device performance data
- Aggregate gameplay statistics

This data is used only to fix problems and improve the Game. It does not
identify you personally.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Dead Orbit X is **not directed to children under 13**, and we do not knowingly
collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided data to us or to our advertising partners,
please contact us at the address in Section 9 and we will act to have it removed.

---

## 6. Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the following rights:

- **Access** — request a copy of data held about you
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your data
- **Restriction and objection** — limit or object to processing
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, without affecting prior processing
- **Lodge a complaint** with your national data protection authority

Our legal basis for personalized advertising is **your consent**, which you give
or refuse on first launch and can change at any time via
**Pause menu → PRIVACY & ADS**.

Because we do not collect personal identifiers ourselves, requests concerning
advertising data are best directed to the advertising partners listed in
Section 2, who act as independent controllers for that data.

---

## 7. Users in California and Other U.S. States

If you decline personalized ads, we signal **"do not sell or share my personal
information"** to our advertising partners on your behalf.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) you may request access to,
deletion of, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
Contact us using Section 9.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data (progress, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

**To delete all data we are involved with:**

1. Uninstall the Game — this removes all locally stored data permanently, or
2. Android → Settings → Apps → Dead Orbit X → Storage → **Clear data**

To delete or reset the advertising identifier held by our advertising partners,
follow the device steps in Section 3, or contact those partners directly.
